# Competitor Analysis

> **Status:** Research in progress — not a feature requirements document.

This document records competitor groups currently under investigation for HuGuWeb product and market research. Competitor products are **research inputs**, not automatic feature requirements.

Do **not** treat competitor capabilities as HuGuWeb scope unless validated through product decision processes. Do **not** fabricate competitor capabilities. Detailed analysis is ongoing; findings should eventually include evidence and sources.

---

## Research Purpose

Competitor research informs:

- Understanding of the hospitality and ERP software landscape
- Identification of common operational patterns in hotel software
- Awareness of integration ecosystems and market expectations
- Differentiation opportunities for HuGuWeb

Competitor research does **not** automatically produce a feature checklist for HuGuWeb.
